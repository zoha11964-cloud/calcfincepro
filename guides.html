Compound interest is one of the most powerful tools for long term savings and wealth building. Unlike simple interest, compound interest earns returns on your principal plus accumulated interest over time. Using a reliable compound interest calculator can help you track your savings growth easily.

Starting your savings early lets compounding work for decades, greatly increasing your final balance. Every regular deposit will amplify the power of compound interest for your future financial goals.
